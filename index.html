For rec softball coaches

Built for 10U–14U teams that have to keep playing time even. Tap a position, pick a player, and the sit-out chart fills itself.

How to put it on your phone

  1. Open the lineup card in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Use it like an app on game day.

What stays private

Player names stay on that phone. This preview does not upload your roster. The PDF you share is the only copy someone else sees.
