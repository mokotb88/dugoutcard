# DugoutCard — Days 1–14

Product name: **DugoutCard**
Promise: Fair playing time. No printer.

Live pages (after GitHub Pages is on):
- Landing: https://mokotb88.github.io/dugoutcard/
- App: https://mokotb88.github.io/dugoutcard/app.html

## Day 1 — You

1. Open the app link on your phone.
2. Safari/Chrome → Add to Home Screen.
3. Enter your real roster and build Saturday’s lineup.
4. Share one PDF to yourself so you know that button works.

## Days 2–3 — Pick 3 coaches

Choose coaches who:
- run rec / rec-plus, not travel-only
- have 11+ kids
- already text you
- will actually use it this weekend

Text them this, unchanged:

---
I built a phone lineup card so I can stop printing.

It shows positions, batting order, and who sits each inning. You share a PDF with the other coach.

Want it free for the rest of this season if you use it at one game and tell me what broke?

Link: https://mokotb88.github.io/dugoutcard/app.html

iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
---

## Days 4–7 — Get them on the field

- Don’t train them for 20 minutes. Stand next to them for 3.
- Goal: they build one real game on their phone.
- After the game, ask only: “What was annoying?” Write it down.

## Days 8–14 — Proof

Take 4 photos on a game night (no close-ups of kids’ faces unless parents are fine with it):
1. Field screen with names
2. Sit-out chart
3. Batting order
4. The PDF in Messages

Ask each tester for one sentence you can use:
“I stopped getting the why-did-she-sit text.”

Do not post in big Facebook groups yet. Finish these 14 days first.

## Done when

- You used it in a real game
- 3 other coaches opened the link
- At least 1 other coach used it in a game
- You have 3 quotes or notes on what to fix
